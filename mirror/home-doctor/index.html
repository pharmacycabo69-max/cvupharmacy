How It Works

FAQ

House-call doctor in Cabo — frequently asked

Quick answers to what most travelers ask before booking a CVU house-call MD.

How fast can a doctor arrive at my hotel in Cabo San Lucas?
Most in-suite MD visits in Cabo San Lucas and the central Corridor are with you within 45–75 minutes of confirming on WhatsApp. Outlying zones like Pedregal, Diamante, Puerto Los Cabos and East Cape may take 60–120 minutes depending on traffic and gated-community access. We confirm the exact ETA before you commit.
Are the doctors licensed?
Yes. Every CVU doctor is a Mexican physician with a current Cédula Profesional (Mexican federal medical license). Our MDs speak working English with travelers and operate within COFEPRIS-authorized scope.
Can the doctor issue medication orders I can fill in Cabo?
Yes. Our MDs issue medication orders on official Mexican letterhead valid at our pharmacy at Plaza Gali and at any licensed pharmacy in Mexico. We can fill the script and deliver it to your hotel room directly, so you don’t need to leave the suite.
Is this a real exam or just a video call?
We offer both. Most travelers book an in-suite house-call exam (physical exam, vitals, history). For minor concerns or triage, we also offer a 15-minute video consult on WhatsApp/Zoom — useful for medication resupply or deciding whether a house call is warranted.
How long does a house-call visit take?
Most house-call visits run 30–60 minutes from arrival to wrap-up. Telemedicine consults are 15 minutes. Pre-surgery consults and complex cases can run 60–90 minutes if needed.
What does a house-call doctor in Cabo cost?
Our menu starts at $79 for a video consult, $200 for a daytime in-suite house call, $295 for an after-hours visit, and up to $249 for a pre-surgery consult — all in USD, all-inclusive of mobile delivery within Cabo, Corridor and San José. Prices subject to change. Lab tests, imaging and IV drips billed separately.
Do you accept insurance?
We don’t bill insurance directly. Most U.S. and Canadian travel-medical policies reimburse out-of-pocket house-call care; we provide an itemized invoice in English with our COFEPRIS information so you can submit for reimbursement after your trip.
What if it’s an emergency?
We do not provide 911-level emergency care. If you have chest pain, severe shortness of breath, severe bleeding, signs of stroke, severe abdominal pain or any life-threatening symptom, call 911 in Mexico or proceed directly to the nearest hospital. For urgent but non-emergency issues, our 24/7 house-call team can usually get an MD to your suite faster than an ER queue.
