Good to know

IV Therapy in Cabo — Frequently Asked

Do I need a doctor’s order for IV therapy?
No special paperwork is needed for our wellness IV drips. Every session does require a free consultation with our licensed physician first — they confirm the drip is suitable for you, adjust the formula if needed, or advise against it when it is not appropriate.
How long does an IV session take?
Most sessions take 45–60 minutes. After your consultation, a licensed nurse typically arrives at your hotel, villa, or yacht within 60–90 minutes — same-day across most of Los Cabos.
Is IV therapy safe?
Our drips are administered by licensed nurses using sterile, single-use, medical-grade supplies, after a physician screening. Your nurse stays with you and monitors the entire session. As with any medical procedure there can be minor risks — that is exactly why we screen first.
Can I get an IV at my hotel, villa, or yacht?
Yes. We serve hotels, private villas, yachts and marinas across Cabo San Lucas, the Tourist Corridor, and San José del Cabo. Your session happens wherever you are comfortable — suite, terrace, or deck.
How much does IV therapy cost in Cabo?
The physician consultation is free. Wellness IV drips range from $165 to $395 USD depending on the formula and add-ons. The exact price is confirmed before you book — no hidden fees. We accept credit cards, USD cash, and wire transfer.
What if I feel unwell during the IV?
Tell your nurse — they remain by your side for the entire session and can slow, pause, or stop the drip at any time. Our physician is on call throughout. For any emergency, call 911 immediately.
Do you take insurance?
We do not bill insurance directly. You receive an itemized receipt in English that you can submit to your travel insurance provider; reimbursement depends on your policy.
How do I book an IV in Cabo?
Tap “Talk to a Doctor” on this page to start your free video consultation, or WhatsApp or call us at +52 624 226 1023. Once the physician confirms your drip, a licensed nurse is dispatched to your location — usually the same day.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes. The video consultation with our licensed physician is included at no extra cost with every IV therapy booking and every pharmacy delivery order — a $150 value. The physician confirms the drip is appropriate for you; there is never a separate consultation charge.
Can I pay in USD?
Yes. Every price is quoted in USD up front on WhatsApp before we dispatch. Pay by credit or debit card, Apple Pay, or cash (USD or MXN) when your nurse arrives — with an itemized receipt in English.
How is IV therapy different from drinking water?
IV drips deliver fluids, electrolytes, and vitamins directly into your bloodstream rather than through digestion, and a licensed nurse tailors the session to you. Whether an IV or simple oral hydration makes more sense for your situation is exactly what the free physician consultation is for.
Can I combine IV therapy with pharmacy delivery?
Absolutely. Your nurse can bring vitamins, supplements, sunscreen, and personal-care items along with your drip — and any pharmacy order also includes the free doctor consultation. Just mention what you need on WhatsApp when you book.
